01 / About us

A simpler way to access services.

ServFex is a service platform focused on connecting customers with local service-provider businesses for everyday home needs. Our goal is to bring service discovery, booking coordination and delivery into one convenient experience.

01 — OUR PURPOSE

Make life easier

Reduce the effort involved in finding, arranging and managing common home services through a more organised service experience.

02 — OUR APPROACH

Connect locally

Build connections between customers and local service-provider businesses, with clearer booking coordination and service information.

03 — OUR COMMITMENT

Keep improving

Develop a platform experience focused on convenience, communication, accountability and continuous improvement.
